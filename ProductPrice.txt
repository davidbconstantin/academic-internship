Conclusion based on my findings

- Most supermarket websites use JavaScript to load their product prices that are displayed
- Some of the common HTML names I found:
  price
  product-price
  current-price
  amount
  value

- Prices are usually inside span or div tags on the websites
- Lidl and Aldi seem cheapest overall for local products which makes sense based on their cost friendly advertising
- Tesco and SuperValu tend to be slightly more expensive
